Trade Union Density
in Latin America

This dashboard presents estimates of trade union density and median monthly earnings across Latin America based on publicly available national surveys (labor/household) data. The countries included are Brazil, Colombia, Guatemala, Mexico, and Peru.

Gaps in the lines indicate years where no survey data were available. Country-level estimates may differ from other sources, as this dashboard applies a harmonized methodology to ensure cross-country comparability (Null membership is treated as non-membership, respondents reported positive, non-zero earnings, and ages 15 to 98). For more information on the methodology, see the accompanying methods document.
