Trade Union Density
in Latin America

This dashboard presents estimates of trade union density across Latin American based on publicly available labor force survey data. The countries included are Brazil, Colombia, Guatemala, Mexico, and Peru.

Data is drawn from national household surveys and covers the period 2004–2024. Figures represent the share of wage and salary earners who are trade union members, disaggregated by sex and age band.

Gaps in the lines indicate years where no survey data was available, most commonly due to Covid-19 disruptions in 2020–2021. Country-level estimates may differ from other sources, as this dashboard applies a harmonized methodology to ensure cross-country comparability (Null membership is treated as non-membership, respondents have positive, non-zero earning, ages 15 to 98).
